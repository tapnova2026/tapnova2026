TapNova product roadmap

The roadmap pairs reliable gameplay with community growth. The dates are proposed targets, not promises.

Q4 2026 · Foundation

Reliable Mini App launch

Improve Telegram authentication, server-backed gameplay, onboarding, error handling, and core performance across mobile and desktop.

Proposed · in progress
H1 2027 · Core game

Retention and real progress

Build dependable daily missions, season progress, energy and spin rules, and rewards that are recorded and verified by the backend.

Planned

Frequently asked questions

What is TapNova?

TapNova is a Telegram Mini App built around the Moon Rush game and its planned TNV ecosystem.

When will the TNV airdrops happen?

The supplied draft lists Q3 2028 for phase 1 and Q4 2030 for phase 2. These are target dates only; participation and allocations are not guaranteed.

Is the TNV token allocation final?

No. The allocation table is transcribed from a planning workbook. Treat it as draft information until final terms are published through official TapNova channels.

Where can I follow TapNova updates?

Open the TapNova Mini App or follow the TapNova Community channel.
