TapNova Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 October 2026

This policy describes the information TapNova may process when you use the Telegram Mini App.

Information processed

  • Telegram account identifier and profile fields Telegram provides, such as first name and username.
  • Game state such as in-app balance, energy, spins, daily claims, activity, and referral relationships.
  • A TON wallet public address if you choose to connect a wallet.
  • Payment request and transaction reference information only if a payment flow is enabled.
  • Basic technical information needed to secure and operate the service.

How it is used

Information is used to authenticate Telegram sessions, save game progress, prevent duplicate rewards and abuse, operate referrals and leaderboards, and check eligibility against published game rules.

Wallet safety

TapNova does not need and must never ask you for your seed phrase or private key. Wallet connection alone does not prove ownership of a payment or mean that a transaction has been verified.

Service providers

The Mini App operates through Telegram, GitHub Pages, Cloudflare Workers/D1, and TON Connect when those services are used. Those providers process information according to their own terms and policies.

Retention and requests

Game records may be retained as needed for account continuity, fraud prevention, and reward integrity. For a privacy question or request, contact @TapNovaEarnBot and include your Telegram user ID; never include passwords, private keys, or seed phrases.

Changes

This policy may be updated as the service changes. The date above indicates the latest stated revision.
