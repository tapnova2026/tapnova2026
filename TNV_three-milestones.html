Token overview

What is TNV?

TNV is the token associated with the TapNova project and its evolving digital ecosystem. TapNova’s direction connects a Telegram gaming experience with longer-term research into AI and Web3 products. The project is exploring whether an AI-concept-led token evolution toward a future TN model could be useful; this is a long-term concept, not a confirmed migration or currently available utility.

This page provides project-level information, not investment advice. Users should verify any token contract address, supply, distribution, and transaction details through official on-chain sources before interacting with a digital asset.

Supply planning

10 Billion TNV

The project’s current planning figure is a maximum supply of 10 billion TNV. Treat this as a project-stated plan until confirmed by the deployed token contract and official on-chain records. Any future TN token model would require separately published supply, conversion, and migration terms.

Blockchain direction

TapNova's intended blockchain ecosystem is TON. A planned ecosystem focus does not by itself prove that a token contract, liquidity pool, or exchange market is live.

Future utility exploration

AI and Web3 utility

Potential future use cases may include access to AI-related services, agent features, or marketplace payments. These are exploratory plans and are not represented as currently available token utilities.

Indicative roadmap

Token and ecosystem milestones

  • 2026 – Foundation: continue development of the TapNova Telegram Mini App and improve core game and backend experiences.
  • 2027 – TON preparation: continue TON ecosystem planning. Q2 2027 is an indicative target for preparing a TNV/TON liquidity-pool initiative, subject to readiness, security checks, and the owner's explicit approval.
  • 2028 – Utility exploration: develop and test possible AI and Web3 utility only where implementation is technically ready and publicly documented. A proposed Airdrop Phase 1 target is Q3 2028, subject to eligibility rules and approval.
  • 2030 – Longer-term expansion: continue ecosystem development. A proposed Airdrop Phase 2 target is Q4 2030, subject to final rules and approval.

All dates are indicative planning targets, not guarantees. The Q4 2030 TN token evolution is a concept milestone—not a third airdrop, confirmed token swap, or promise of new utility. No liquidity pool, listing, airdrop, token allocation, or market availability is guaranteed. No blockchain transaction should be made without the owner’s explicit approval.
