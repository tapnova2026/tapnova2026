The TapNova journey: three big milestones

Every season gives players a new chapter to explore. These milestones guide the long-term journey—with progress, rules, and important changes shared transparently.

Q4 2026 · Build the foundation

🚀 Moon Rush gets stronger

Improve the Telegram Mini App, secure player accounts, dependable game records, faster gameplay, and clear onboarding. The first goal: make every session smooth and player progress reliable.

Foundation · development and testing
H1 2027 · Make every season count

🎯 Missions, levels & collections

Grow daily and weekly missions, season goals, XP progression, sticker albums, character unlocks, Wheel, Shop, and in-game achievements. Give players fresh goals throughout every season.

Planned · feature readiness dependent
Q2 2027 · Prepare the TON path

💎 TNV and the TON ecosystem

Prepare the technical and security work for a possible TNV/TON liquidity-pool initiative. Any pool creation or on-chain action requires readiness checks and the project owner’s explicit approval.

Indicative target · not a live pool announcement
H2 2027 · Grow together

🤝 Friends, referrals & community events

Improve referral attribution and anti-abuse protection, introduce community challenges and fair leaderboards, and celebrate player milestones. Reward rules will be published before each campaign.

Planned
Q1 2028 · Milestone 01

🎁 First community Airdrop

Target the first proposed TNV airdrop in Q1 2028. Before distribution, publish eligibility criteria, activity and snapshot rules, anti-bot checks, allocation methodology, claim steps, and timing.

Target · Q1 2028 · not guaranteed
2028 · Build utility with care

🛡️ Security, transparency & utility

Continue security reviews and clear token documentation while researching practical AI and Web3 use cases. Any utility will be described as available only after it is implemented, tested, and documented.

Long-term development
Q2 2029 · Milestone 02

🌟 Second community Airdrop

Target a second proposed TNV airdrop in Q2 2029. It may recognize eligible participation across the evolving TapNova ecosystem, subject to published rules, project readiness, and approval. Criteria may differ from phase one.

Target · Q2 2029 · not guaranteed
2029–2030 · The AI chapter

🧠 Build the AI-enabled TapNova vision

Research and test AI-powered experiences such as helpful game agents, smarter player experiences, and useful Web3-connected services. These are concepts to validate—not features promised to be live today.

Exploration · feasibility and testing dependent
Q4 2030 · Milestone 03

✨ Proposed TN token evolution

Explore an AI-concept-led evolution or upgrade of TNV toward a future TN token model. The name, token mechanics, migration or swap process, supply treatment, utility, and security design must be researched and publicly explained before any decision or on-chain action.

Vision target · Q4 2030 · subject to approval

Frequently asked questions

What is TapNova?

TapNova is a Telegram Mini App built around the Moon Rush game and its planned TNV ecosystem.

When are the proposed airdrop milestones?

The current planning targets are Q1 2028 for the first airdrop and Q2 2029 for the second. Dates, eligibility, and allocations are not guaranteed and will require separately published rules.

Is the Q4 2030 milestone a third airdrop?

No. The third milestone is a proposed AI-concept-led evolution or upgrade of TNV toward a future TN token model. It is a research and planning target, not a confirmed token migration or airdrop.

Will players need to do anything for a token upgrade?

No action should be taken based on this roadmap alone. If a token evolution is approved, TapNova will publish verified instructions, contract details, security information, and any migration process through confirmed official channels.

Is the TNV token allocation final?

No. The allocation table is transcribed from a planning workbook. Treat it as draft information until final terms are published through official TapNova channels.

Where can I follow TapNova updates?

Follow the TapNova Community channel for confirmed updates and official links.
